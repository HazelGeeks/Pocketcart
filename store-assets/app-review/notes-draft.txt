2. Purpose and target audience
PocketCart helps shoppers at supported Canadian grocery stores compare available prices, plan grocery shopping, and track food at home. It combines grocery offers, a shopping cart, free product alerts, fridge/freezer inventory and optional receipt records. Prices and availability vary by retailer, location and date. PocketCart does not sell groceries or process grocery orders.

3. Access and main features
Browse Home without an account. Open a product for store prices and add it to Cart; Cart supports up to 10 distinct products. Sign in from Account using the review credentials in the App Review Information sign-in fields. Account creation is also available there.
Product alerts are free. My Freezer stores food names, quantities, locations and optional best-before dates.
Account > Features contains Map, Scan and Receipts. Map supports city/postal-code search. Receipts supports manual entry or a receipt photo; users review extracted fields before saving. Scan and receipt extraction ask for explicit confirmation before sending a photo to OpenAI. No sample file is required for basic use; a synthetic receipt with Milk CAD 4.00, Apples CAD 3.00 and Total CAD 7.00 can demonstrate receipt entry.
Account > Family provides invitation-only Cart/My Freezer sharing. Owners can remove members and cancel invitations; members can leave. Receipts remain private to the account. There is no public feed or public messaging, and no separate in-app reporting/blocking interface.
Account > Account actions > Delete Account provides account deletion.
Build 18 does not offer new subscription purchases. Product alerts, My Freezer and expiry reminders are free. Optional Ko-fi support does not unlock digital content or app features.
