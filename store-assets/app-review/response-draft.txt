2. Purpose and audience
PocketCart helps shoppers at supported Canadian grocery stores compare available prices, plan shopping, and track food at home. Coverage and prices vary by retailer, location and date. It does not sell groceries or process grocery orders.

3. Access and features
Browse Home without an account. Cart supports up to 10 distinct products. Product alerts, food storage and expiry reminders are free.
Sign in from Account with the dedicated review credentials in App Review Information. Account > Features contains Map and Receipts. Food Scan is temporarily unavailable. Map supports city/postal-code search without location access. Receipts supports manual entry and optional photos; users confirm OpenAI photo extraction and review the result before saving.
Account > Family provides invitation-only Cart/food-inventory sharing, with removal, invitation cancellation and leaving. Receipts remain personal. There is no public feed or public messaging.
Account > Account actions > Delete Account removes the account. The subscription screen retains Restore purchases and Manage subscription for existing users; it offers no new purchase button. There are no external donations or payment links.
